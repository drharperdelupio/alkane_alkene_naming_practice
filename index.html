Alkane and Alkene Naming and Structure Practice

This page is optional and ungraded. It does not save your progress, so nothing is recorded if you leave or refresh.

  1. Part 1 — Name to structure: read the name and draw the skeletal (bond-line) structure.
  2. Part 2 — Structure to name: look at the skeletal structure and type its name.

Alkanes, cycloalkanes and alkenes are all included. For alkene names, either style is accepted: the newer IUPAC style puts the locant in front of the ending (hex-2-ene) and the older style puts it in front of the whole name (2-hexene). Where a double bond can be E or Z, include it, for example (E)-hex-2-ene.

Tips for the drawing tool
  • Pick the bond tool, then click and drag on the canvas. Each end of a line is a carbon. Click an existing bond with the bond tool to change it to a double bond.
  • Do not draw hydrogens on carbon. They are understood.
  • The checker compares connectivity. It checks E or Z only for the problems whose name includes E or Z.
